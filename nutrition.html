Weekly Nutrition Note

PROTEIN: Building Blocks for Muscle Growth

This week, the focus is on protein. Protein is essential for muscle repair, recovery, and growth. It also helps keep you full and satisfied, which can support your overall nutrition goals.

A helpful structure is protein first, then produce, then a smart carb or healthy fat depending on your needs and training day. That could look like eggs with fruit and toast in the morning, a chicken bowl at lunch, and salmon, rice, and vegetables at dinner.

If your week gets busy, simplify instead of skipping. A Greek yogurt bowl, protein smoothie, rotisserie chicken. The goal is to keep protein consistent, even if the rest of the meal is simplified.

Coach cue for the week

Before each meal, ask: “Where is my protein? Am I getting enough?” If the answer is no, add a protein source to your meal or snack.

Member Notes

Useful for quick reminders, grocery prep ideas, and flexible substitutions.

Grocery Focus

Keep protein easy: eggs, Greek yogurt, rotisserie chicken, turkey, salmon, tuna packets, protein powder.

Carb Options

Really focus on complex carbs: brown rice, quinoa, sweet potatoes, whole wheat bread, oats, beans. Avoid simple carbs like white bread, pastries, and sugary cereals.

Simple Substitutions

  • Swap chicken for turkey or salmon.
  • Swap rice for potatoes, wraps, or quinoa.
  • Swap yogurt for cottage cheese or a protein shake.
  • Swap vegetables based on preference and availability.
