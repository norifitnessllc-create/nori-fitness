In-Person Training Rundown

Direct coaching, real-time feedback, and structured sessions designed for clients who want hands-on support.

Important Program Notes

Review these details before booking your consultation.

01

Exclusive to the DMV / NOVA area

In-person training is currently available only in DC, Maryland, and Virginia / Northern Virginia.

02

All sessions take place in public spaces

No in-home training sessions are offered.

03

Gym locations must allow outside trainers

04

Approved building gyms are allowed

Gyms or training facilities at commercial, apartment, and condo buildings may be used in accordance with property rules.
