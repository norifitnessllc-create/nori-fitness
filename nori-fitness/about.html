My Story

Fitness built from lived experience, not just theory.

Welcome, my name is Nahari and I am the founder and coach behind Nori-Fitness LLC. My approach to fitness is shaped by personal experience, not just certifications. I’ve lived with chronic autoimmune conditions such as Ulcerative Colitis and Asthma.

Managing these challenges taught me early on that fitness isn’t about extremes — it’s about consistency, adaptability, and listening to your body. Training has to work with your health, not against it.

Through years of trial, learning, and discipline, I discovered how powerful it is to have balanced and appropriate nutrition alongside smart training. Fueling the body correctly doesn’t just improve performance — it supports recovery, energy levels, and long-term health.

Today, I help clients train in a way that’s sustainable, realistic, and results-driven. My goal isn’t just to help you look stronger — it’s to help you feel confident, capable, and in control of your health.

Coaching Values

The principles behind how I coach and support clients.

Sustainable Progress
