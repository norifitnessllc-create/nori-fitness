In-Person Coaching

Hands-on coaching with direct guidance, structure, and accountability.

In-person training is built for clients who want real-time coaching, consistent scheduling, and a clear progression plan delivered face to face.

Availability & Location

Review service area and training setup before booking your consultation.

Current Service Area

In-person training is currently only available in the DC, Maryland, and Virginia (Northern Virginia) region.

Training Environment

Sessions take place in approved public training spaces, gyms that allow outside trainers, or approved residential/commercial building gyms where permitted.

What In-Person Training Includes

A higher-touch coaching experience built around direct support, clear progression, and consistency.

Real-Time Form Coaching

Get immediate adjustments, setup correction, and movement feedback during each session.

Structured Progression

Train with a plan that builds over time instead of guessing from session to session.

Built-In Accountability

Stay consistent through scheduled sessions and a coaching structure designed to keep you on track.

Scheduling Expectations

In-person coaching works best when expectations are clear from the start.

Fixed Scheduling

Your session times are set based on availability and your selected plan. Requested times are not guaranteed until approved.

Consistency Matters

Progress depends on showing up, following the plan, and maintaining regular session attendance.

Rescheduling Is Limited

Schedule changes may be limited based on availability and notice. Missed sessions may be shortened, forfeited, or rescheduled at discretion.

Location Approval Required

Training locations must be workable, approved, and aligned with facility or property rules before sessions begin.
